Terms marked with 🔒 are canonical — they are officially defined technical terms in this project's vocabulary. They appear consistently across documentation and code.

## ⚠️ Translation Notes

This glossary was carefully compiled with attention to technical precision. Key considerations:

- **עימוד** is translated as **"typesetting"** (not just "layout") — it specifically refers to the typographic process of arranging text for print.
- **יישור** is translated as **"justification"** (not "alignment") — in this typographic context, it refers to full justification of text.
- **כותרות עליונות** is translated as **"running headers"** (not "top headings") — these are repeating page-top headings, a standard book-printing term.
- **אלמנות/יתומות** are translated as **"widows/orphans"** — standard typographic terms with specific meanings (widow = last line at top of next column; orphan = first line at bottom of previous column).
- **חלון מילה ראשונה** is translated as **"drop cap / initial window"** — literally "first word window", the enlarged first word/letter at a paragraph start.
- **מרזב** is translated as **"gutter"** — the space between columns (not "gutter" as in a rain gutter).
- **תגים** in font context means **"serifs"**; in markup context means **"tags"** — context determines meaning.
- **שער** is translated as **"gate (title page)"** — literally "gate", but in book context it means a title page.
- **ברך** is kept as **"Berech"** — a project-specific term for an L-shaped layout (literally "knee").
